# Combined emissions comparison

Emissions responses in 2050, with NDC on the left and NDC+LTT on the right. Panels a,b reproduce the European all-industry GHG comparison for medium/high EU-only CE, pairing solid OMNIA-ENGAGE bars on the left with hatched GCAM bars on the right. OMNIA selects its reported all-industry parent Emissions|GHG|Industry, including sectors beyond cement, iron and steel, and aluminium. GCAM sums Emissions|Kyoto Gases|Energy|Demand|Industry and Emissions|Kyoto Gases|Industrial Processes. OMNIA sums EUE, EUM and EUW; GCAM selects EU-27 once. These regional boundaries differ slightly. Panels c,d compare global steel responses, pairing OMNIA-ENGAGE CE in the EU plus partners (EU+) with OPEN-PROM medium/high scenarios. The global output scope is distinct from the policy adoption area. OPEN-PROM's intervention definitions and adoption area are undocumented, so medium/high labels do not establish policy equivalence. OMNIA sums all 28 model regions and selects reported steel GHG; OPEN-PROM selects World and steel energy-related CO2. OPEN-PROM has no steel-specific process-emissions or GHG total, and its energy-related CO2 is not harmonised to OMNIA's reported steel GHG. All bars show 100 × (scenario emissions − own reference emissions) / own reference emissions, using the same model, climate pathway, geography, emissions definition and year for numerator and denominator. References are each model's own no-CE/reference case; no absolute-level alignment is assumed. The rows have different geography, sector scope, policy coverage and emissions inventories. Percentage scales match within each row and are independent between rows. Gas coverage, GWP conventions and process boundaries remain source-defined. Reported negative emissions and signed increases are retained; carbon capture is not subtracted again. Endpoint markers identify observed values, including zero, and signed labels are rounded to 0.1%. Missing responses remain gaps. Original source quantities, references, variables, regions, units and missing treatments are retained in the source data. European decompositions and focused three-sector calculations remain unplotted context, identified by PlotRole=context; only industry net rows are plotted in the upper row. The lower row excludes OMNIA's EU-only CE cases.
