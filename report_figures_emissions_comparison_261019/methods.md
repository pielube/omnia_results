# Combined emissions comparison methods

Regenerate with `python -m omnia_results --config figures.openprom_comparison.261016.json`. The OPEN-PROM config names the European comparison config and the separate combined-output directory.

The top row compares the combined cement, iron-and-steel and aluminium emissions response at 2050 for EU-only medium/high CE in both models. Other industry is excluded from the plotted totals. OMNIA sums its three reported Emissions|GHG|Industry subsector totals in MtCO2e/yr. GCAM sums four exact variables in Mt CO2/yr: Emissions|CO2|Energy|Demand|Industry|Non-Metallic Minerals; Emissions|CO2|Industrial Processes|Non-Metallic Minerals|Cement; Emissions|CO2|Energy|Demand|Industry|Iron and Steel; and Emissions|CO2|Energy|Demand|Industry|Non-Ferrous Metals|Aluminum. The non-metallic-minerals energy category is used as a cement-energy proxy: its reported final-energy parent equals the cement child in the supplied workbook, but this does not establish a documented cement-exclusive emissions inventory. Cement process CO2 is added once; overlapping industry, non-ferrous-metals and process parents are excluded. The export provides no additional steel/aluminium process-CO2 children or sector Kyoto-gas totals. The GCAM CO2 scope therefore remains distinct from OMNIA GHG. Absolute sector quantities are summed first, then compared with the same model's combined three-sector no-CE reference; sector percentages are not averaged. GCAM is read from `DIAMOND_8p7p3_industry_19102026_standardized_v2.xlsx`, as configured in `figures.europe_relative.261016.json`.

The bottom row compares global steel under OMNIA EU+ and OPEN-PROM medium/high settings: OMNIA sector GHG versus OPEN-PROM steel energy-related CO2. Both rows use their original source adapters, scenarios, exact units and own-model same-year references. Gas/process boundaries are not harmonised. EU/EU+ denotes policy adoption; output geography differs between the rows. The percentage scales match within each row and are independent between rows. Only the OMNIA EU+ cases enter the lower row. The OPEN-PROM intervention and adoption geography remain unspecified.

Original absolute quantities and denominator provenance accompany every bar. PlotValue/PlotUnit identify percentages; Value/Unit retain each input adapter's original row definition. Plotted and PlotRole distinguish the sixteen plotted bars from reference and decomposition context. ComparisonRow and Panel identify the figure's two distinct scopes. Carbon capture is not subtracted again. Missing activity retains the configurations' provisional zero assumption for OMNIA. In particular, NDC+LTT aluminium GHG is blank in all three OMNIA EU regions for no/medium/high CE and is provisionally treated as zero; this does not establish physical zero emissions. Unavailable responses remain gaps. Source hashes and both configurations are recorded in audit.json. All-industry totals and sector decompositions remain unplotted context. This combined export writes only its own output directory.
