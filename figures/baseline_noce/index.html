Definitions and data checks
  • Only baseline_noce is plotted. Source scenarios: baseline_noce, ndc_noce.
  • Global quantities sum the 28 configured source regions, assumed to form a disjoint world partition. No global aggregate is supplied in the CSV.
  • Blank cells and absent regional rows are kept missing. Global sums require every region; incomplete totals appear as gaps. Prices, costs and reported intensities always retain missing values. The missing-input ledger records every affected input.
  • European production is shown separately for ENE, ENW, EUE, EUM, EUW. Region names are not expanded without a model codebook.
  • Regional sets contain at most seven regions in source-code order for legibility; these sets do not represent geographic aggregates.
  • Cement production is the cement-sector intensity denominator. Steel and aluminium production are primary plus secondary; cement and clinker are not summed.
  • Global energy and emissions intensities are ratios of summed quantities, never unweighted averages of regional intensities.
  • Reported regional energy intensities differ from the document's energy/production calculation by more than 1% in 220 of 228 comparable region-sector-years. Both definitions are retained and exported in intensity_comparison.csv.
  • The source contains 14 negative regional cost observations. All are preserved (negative_costs.csv). Global steel cost exceeds the global system total in 2019; check the source cost accounting before interpreting this comparison.
  • No smoothing, extrapolation, uncertainty bands, clipping of negative data, or normalization to an arbitrary base year is applied.
