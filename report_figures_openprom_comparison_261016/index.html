Combined four-panel GCAM and OPEN-PROM emissions comparison

Every quantity is global. EU/EU+ identifies OMNIA's CE adoption area, not the area being summed. OPEN-PROM's medium/high intervention and adoption geography are undocumented; the labels do not establish equivalent policies.
Emissions scopes differ: OMNIA reports steel GHG; OPEN-PROM reports steel energy-related CO₂. Its export has no matching steel process-emissions or GHG total. Percentage responses retain this difference. OMNIA missing activity follows the provisional zero assumption.
