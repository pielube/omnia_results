Comparing the response to circular economy

Three figures show 2050 percentage changes from each model's own no-CE case: production, final energy, and emissions. The emissions figure compares reported all-industry responses in two panels, with NDC on the left and NDC+LTT on the right. Both pathways retain medium/high EU-only CE.

No CE is 0% by construction within each model, pathway and reference scope. This normalisation does not establish agreement in absolute levels or harmonise process boundaries. OMNIA's emissions bars use its reported all-industry parent, including sectors beyond cement, steel and aluminium. GCAM uses industry energy-demand plus industrial-process Kyoto gases and has no matching three-sector GHG totals. Aluminium production and energy process coverage remain unconfirmed.
